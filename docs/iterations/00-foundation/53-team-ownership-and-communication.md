# Iteration 00 - Team Ownership And Communication

## Communication Goal
Keep foundation decisions explicit across product, backend, mobile and source policy.

## Decision Owners
- Product owner: product loop and glossary.
- Architecture owner: bounded context map.
- Source policy owner: source acquisition policy.
- Contract owner: REST/event standards.

## Reviewers
- Backend lead reviews context and contract impact.
- Mobile lead reviews client-facing terminology and contract expectations.
- Operations representative reviews source risk and support impact.

## Sync Points
- Kickoff: align loop, vocabulary and constraints.
- Midpoint: review unresolved assumptions.
- Closeout: approve handoff into platform work.

## Escalate When
- A source path cannot be classified.
- A term has conflicting meanings.
- A context has overlapping ownership.
- A contract rule is not implementable.

## Handoff Message
Foundation is ready when Iteration 01 can create platform tickets without redefining vocabulary, source policy or contract rules.
