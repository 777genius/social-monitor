# Iteration 00 - Execution Calendar And Cadence

## Cadence Goal
Keep foundation work moving without allowing vague decisions to survive until closeout.

## Kickoff
- Confirm product loop, glossary owner, context-map owner and source-policy owner.
- Review sprint-zero bootstrap and start blockers.
- Agree where ADR seeds will be recorded.

## Midpoint
- Review glossary conflicts.
- Review bounded-context ownership.
- Review source policy examples.
- Review contract examples for tenant scope, versioning and idempotency.

## Review
- Walk through foundation review board questions.
- Confirm implementation tickets can be cut.
- Validate no source path bypasses risk classification.

## Closeout
- Fill go/no-go decision.
- Complete handoff to Iteration 01.
- Record residual risks with owner and deadline.

## Stop Conditions
- Core terms conflict across teams.
- Source policy remains unowned.
- Contract standards cannot guide platform tickets.
