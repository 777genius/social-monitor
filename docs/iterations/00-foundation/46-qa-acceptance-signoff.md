# Iteration 00 - QA Acceptance Signoff

## Signoff Goal
Confirm that foundation decisions are testable, reviewable and ready to drive implementation.

## Acceptance Scenarios
- Product loop can be explained end to end.
- Glossary terms map to bounded contexts.
- Source policy can approve, restrict or block a source.
- Contract standards produce usable REST/event examples.

## Negative Cases
- Source has no risk class.
- Context has no owner.
- Contract example has no tenant scope or version.
- Personal MVP assumption removes future multi-tenancy.

## Regression Coverage
- Glossary snapshot.
- Context map snapshot.
- Source policy examples.
- Contract/event examples.

## Residual Risks
- Some provider limits may remain estimated.
- Future source roadmap may remain unranked.

## Approvers
- Product owner.
- Architecture owner.
- Source policy owner.
- Contract owner.
