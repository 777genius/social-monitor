# Iteration 00 - Final Go/No-Go Checklist

## Decision Scope
Decide whether foundation is ready to promote into platform implementation.

## Go Conditions
- Product loop and glossary are accepted.
- Bounded contexts have owners.
- Source policy has allowed, restricted and blocked examples.
- Contract standards include tenant scope, versioning and idempotency.
- First platform tickets can be created without redefining terms.

## Hold Conditions
- Source policy owner is missing.
- Any context has disputed ownership.
- Contract rules are too vague for implementation.

## Rework Conditions
- Multi-tenancy was removed from the MVP model.
- A source path was approved without risk classification.
- Product loop cannot be traced to implementation tickets.

## Accepted Exceptions
- Future source ranking can remain open.
- Provider-specific limits can move to connector iteration.

## Critical Audit Evidence
- Tenant ownership, source policy and contract examples are recorded.
- Future source requests have readiness path and owner.
- No implementation ticket depends on an unclassified source path.

## Decision Record
Record decision as `go`, `hold` or `rework` with owner, date, evidence links and next action.
