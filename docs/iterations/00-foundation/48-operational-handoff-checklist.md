# Iteration 00 - Operational Handoff Checklist

## Handoff Goal
Transfer foundation decisions to platform implementation without hidden product or architecture assumptions.

## Owners To Hand Off
- Product vocabulary owner.
- Bounded context owner.
- Source policy owner.
- Contract standards owner.

## Assets To Hand Off
- Product loop and glossary.
- Bounded context map.
- Source acquisition policy.
- REST/event contract standards.
- Ticket quality rule.

## Known Issues
- Future source roadmap may still be unranked.
- Some provider limits may be estimates until adapters are built.
- Exact infrastructure tooling choices may move to Iteration 01.

## Support Impact
- Support should understand known source-policy constraints.
- Support should not promise blocked or unreviewed source integrations.

## Acceptance
Iteration 01 owner accepts handoff only when vocabulary, source policy and contract standards are clear enough to create implementation tickets.
