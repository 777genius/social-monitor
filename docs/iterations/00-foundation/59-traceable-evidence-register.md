# Iteration 00 - Traceable Evidence Register

## Evidence Goal
Prove that foundation decisions are accepted, owned and usable by implementation.

## Critical Audit Evidence
- Tenant ownership language exists for aggregates and contracts.
- Source policy classifies allowed, restricted and blocked acquisition paths.
- Contract examples include tenant scope, versioning and idempotency where relevant.
- Unsupported source requests have readiness/profile path, not implementation approval.

## Decision Evidence
- Product loop: approved diagram or document link.
- Glossary: reviewed vocabulary snapshot.
- Bounded contexts: owner-approved context map.
- Source policy: allowed/restricted/blocked source examples.
- Contract standards: REST/event examples with tenant scope and versioning.

## Ticket Evidence
- Foundation tickets reference glossary terms.
- Contract tickets reference standards.
- Source policy tickets include risk class.

## Review Evidence
- Cross-functional review board approval.
- ADR seeds created for architectural decisions.
- Scope-change decisions recorded where relevant.

## Handoff Evidence
- Iteration 01 owner accepts platform-start prerequisites.
- Open assumptions have owner and decision date.

## Missing Evidence Blocks
- Unowned context.
- Unreviewed source policy.
- Contract standard without implementation example.
