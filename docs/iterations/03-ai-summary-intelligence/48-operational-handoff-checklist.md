# Iteration 03 - Operational Handoff Checklist

## Handoff Goal
Transfer cited summary behavior to mobile and realtime delivery.

## Owners To Hand Off
- SummaryPolicy owner.
- Citation/evidence owner.
- AI provider-port owner.
- Eval owner.
- Cost telemetry owner.

## Assets To Hand Off
- SummaryPolicy schema.
- Citation and evidence examples.
- Summary status/failure contract.
- Eval harness results.
- Cost telemetry samples.

## Known Issues
- Larger eval datasets can grow after beta.
- Additional providers can be added later through the port.
- Advanced personalization may remain deferred.

## Support Impact
- Support should understand cited summary trust model.
- Product should know which summary failures are expected and user-visible.

## Acceptance
Iteration 04 owner accepts handoff only when mobile can render summary, citations and failure states clearly.
