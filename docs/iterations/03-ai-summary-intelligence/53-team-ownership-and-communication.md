# Iteration 03 - Team Ownership And Communication

## Communication Goal
Keep summary quality, evidence, provider choices and mobile trust expectations aligned.

## Decision Owners
- AI lead: provider port, prompt/eval strategy and summary policy.
- Evidence owner: citation model.
- Backend lead: summary API and events.
- Operations owner: cost telemetry.

## Reviewers
- Mobile lead reviews citation display and failure states.
- Product owner reviews summary formats.
- QA owner reviews eval and negative cases.

## Sync Points
- Kickoff: confirm policy and citation requirements.
- Midpoint: review eval failures and telemetry.
- Closeout: confirm mobile contract readiness.

## Escalate When
- A summary can be useful but uncited.
- Provider behavior requires public contract change.
- Cost cannot be attributed.
- Mobile cannot explain summary trust.

## Handoff Message
Summary intelligence is ready when mobile can display cited summaries, trust evidence and failure states without provider-specific assumptions.
