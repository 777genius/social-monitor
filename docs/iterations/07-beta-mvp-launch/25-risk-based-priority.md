# Iteration 07 - Risk-Based Priority

## Priority 1 - Scope Freeze

- Risk: Beta becomes unstable because sources/features keep changing.
- Do First: Freeze supported sources and workflows.
- Do Not Defer: Known limitations.

## Priority 2 - Support Diagnosis

- Risk: Beta failures cannot be understood without developers.
- Do First: Runbooks, dashboards and triage categories.

## Priority 3 - Feedback Taxonomy

- Risk: Beta produces anecdotes, not roadmap evidence.
- Do First: Categorize source requests, summary quality and UX issues.

## Priority 4 - Source Expansion Discipline

- Risk: High-risk sources are added due to pressure.
- Do First: Require capability profile, risk class and beta evidence.
