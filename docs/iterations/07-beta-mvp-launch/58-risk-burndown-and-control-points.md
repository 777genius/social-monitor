# Iteration 07 - Risk Burndown And Control Points

## Burndown Goal
Reduce launch, support and roadmap-quality risk before post-MVP expansion.

## Day 1 Control Point
- Beta scope is frozen.
- Supported sources are confirmed.
- Launch/pause owners are named.

## Midpoint Control Point
- Onboarding walkthrough is tested.
- Known limitations are visible.
- Feedback taxonomy is ready.

## Closeout Control Point
- Beta findings are classified.
- Source expansion requests have demand/risk/cost evidence.
- Post-MVP backlog preserves architecture guardrails.

## Escalation Threshold
Escalate if launch cannot be paused, onboarding fails or unsupported sources enter active scope.

## Residual Risk Rule
Post-MVP feature demand may carry forward; launch safety and evidence-quality risks may not.
