# Iteration 07 - Operational Runbook

## Daily Workflow

1. Review beta scope and known limitations.
2. Check onboarding completion.
3. Monitor scan success and summary usefulness.
4. Triage feedback into product, source, summary, UX or ops category.
5. Track unsupported source requests.
6. Update launch notes and support runbook.
7. Review open incidents by tenant/topic/source and user-visible status.
8. Check whether repeated feedback requires docs, UX copy, source roadmap or product change.

## Review Cadence

- Daily beta health review.
- Twice-weekly feedback review.
- Weekly source priority review.
- Launch retrospective after beta cohort.

## Blockers

- Beta users cannot complete full loop.
- Support cannot diagnose common failures.
- Provider quota blocks onboarding.
- Feedback is not tied to context.
- Known limitations are unclear.
- Incident repeats without owner or mitigation.
- Users cannot tell whether a failure is source outage, quota, empty feed, summary failure or app issue.
- Source request lacks demand evidence or production-safe access path.

## Handoff Notes

- Hand off feedback evidence to roadmap planning.
- Hand off source requests to source research/adapters lane.
- Hand off recurring support issues to product and UX.
- Hand off operational incidents to hardening backlog.

## Support And Ops Impact

- Beta is a learning loop, not just a release.
- Every support issue should map to tenant, topic, source binding, scan, feed item or summary.
- Next-source decisions must be based on observed demand and production-safe acquisition options.
- Every support response should classify the issue and record whether it changes MVP scope, documentation, source roadmap or hardening backlog.
