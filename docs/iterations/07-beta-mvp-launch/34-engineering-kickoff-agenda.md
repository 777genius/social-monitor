# Iteration 07 - Engineering Kickoff Agenda

## Meeting Goal
Launch a controlled beta that validates the full MVP loop and converts feedback into roadmap decisions.

## Required Attendees
- Product owner.
- Engineering lead.
- Support/on-call owner.
- QA owner.
- Operations owner.

## Agenda
1. Confirm beta scope freeze and supported sources.
2. Confirm onboarding and known limitations.
3. Confirm launch checklist and rollback triggers.
4. Confirm support triage and feedback taxonomy.
5. Confirm success metrics and post-beta decision process.

## Decisions To Lock
- Beta cohort and access rules.
- Launch go/no-go criteria.
- Rollback and pause triggers.
- Roadmap decision cadence after beta feedback.

## Edge Cases To Discuss
- Beta user requests an unsupported source.
- A supported source fails during launch week.
- Feedback conflicts across user profiles.
- Metrics look healthy but qualitative trust is low.

## First-Day Output
- Beta scope document is final.
- Launch checklist owner is assigned.
- Support triage path is ready.
- Success metrics dashboard is reviewed.
