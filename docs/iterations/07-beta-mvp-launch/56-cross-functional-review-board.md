# Iteration 07 - Cross-Functional Review Board

## Review Goal
Approve beta closeout and post-MVP direction using evidence, not scope pressure.

## Required Reviewers
- Product owner.
- Engineering lead.
- Support owner.
- Operations owner.
- Source policy owner.
- Architecture owner.

## Review Questions
- Did beta validate the core MVP loop?
- Are source requests classified by demand, risk and cost?
- Are known limitations and support findings captured?
- Are post-MVP backlog items architecture-safe?
- Are ADRs and quality gates updated from beta learning?

## Required Evidence
- Beta metrics summary.
- Feedback taxonomy report.
- Launch/rollback notes.
- Known limitations.
- Post-MVP backlog/ADR candidates.

## Approval Rule
Close MVP beta only when findings are converted into prioritized, architecture-safe post-MVP work.
