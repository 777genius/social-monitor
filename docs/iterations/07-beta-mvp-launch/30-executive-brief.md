# Iteration 07 - Executive Brief

## Goal

Launch a controlled beta that validates the core loop and produces evidence for next product/source priorities.

## Main Risk

Beta scope expands into unsupported sources or unmeasurable feedback before the MVP loop is proven.

## Required Outputs

- Scope freeze.
- Supported source list.
- Known limitations.
- Onboarding and support runbook.
- Launch and rollback checklist.
- Feedback taxonomy.
- Beta metrics dashboard.

## Stop Gate

Do not expand sources until beta demand and production-safe acquisition path both exist.

## Next Transition

Use beta evidence to decide the next source, hardening or product iteration.
