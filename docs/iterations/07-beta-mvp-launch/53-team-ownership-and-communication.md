# Iteration 07 - Team Ownership And Communication

## Communication Goal
Keep launch, support, feedback and post-MVP roadmap decisions aligned.

## Decision Owners
- Product owner: beta scope and roadmap classification.
- Engineering lead: technical go/no-go.
- Support owner: triage and known limitations.
- Operations owner: launch and rollback evidence.

## Reviewers
- Source policy owner reviews source expansion requests.
- QA owner reviews onboarding and launch scenarios.
- Architecture owner reviews post-MVP decisions.

## Sync Points
- Kickoff: confirm scope freeze.
- Launch checkpoint: review go/no-go.
- Closeout: convert beta evidence into roadmap.

## Escalate When
- Unsupported source enters launch discussion.
- Rollback owner is unclear.
- Users cannot complete onboarding.
- Feedback suggests architecture change.

## Handoff Message
Beta is ready to close when findings are converted into prioritized backlog, ADRs and updated quality gates.
