# Iteration 07 - Production Readiness Gap Analysis

## Readiness Goal
Ensure beta launch produces validated learning without uncontrolled scope or unsupported production promises.

## MVP-Ready Areas
- Beta scope is frozen.
- Supported sources are clear.
- Known limitations are published.
- Rollback/pause triggers exist.
- Feedback maps to roadmap decisions.

## Acceptable MVP Gaps
- Broad source catalog can wait.
- Advanced team administration can wait.
- Enterprise reporting can wait.

## Blocking Gaps
- Users cannot complete core onboarding.
- Launch cannot be paused safely.
- Unsupported sources bypass policy.
- Feedback has no owner or classification.

## Owner Actions
- Product owner fixes scope and roadmap gaps.
- Engineering lead fixes launch readiness gaps.
- Support owner fixes triage gaps.
- Operations owner fixes rollback evidence gaps.

## Follow-Up
Post-MVP expansion should start from beta evidence, source-risk analysis and updated quality gates.
