# Iteration 07 - Operational Handoff Checklist

## Handoff Goal
Transfer beta findings into post-MVP roadmap, support knowledge and architecture decisions.

## Owners To Hand Off
- Product roadmap owner.
- Engineering roadmap owner.
- Support owner.
- Operations owner.
- Source expansion owner.

## Assets To Hand Off
- Beta scope and known limitations.
- Launch/rollback execution notes.
- Feedback taxonomy report.
- Metrics dashboard summary.
- Post-MVP backlog and ADR candidates.

## Known Issues
- Broad source catalog remains future roadmap.
- Enterprise features remain post-MVP.
- Some trust/relevance work may need deeper eval cycles.

## Support Impact
- Support should know which limitations remain after beta.
- Product should know which source requests are demand-backed and policy-safe.

## Acceptance
Post-MVP owners accept handoff only when beta evidence is converted into prioritized backlog, ADRs and quality-gate updates.
