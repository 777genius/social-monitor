# Iteration 04 - Executive Brief

## Goal

Build the Flutter MVP flow with feature-scoped Clean Architecture, MobX stores and clear operational states.

## Main Risk

The app looks usable but cannot complete the full loop or hides source/scan/summary failures.

## Required Outputs

- Flutter shell.
- Feature-scoped structure.
- Generated REST client adapters.
- Topic/source/feed/summary features.
- Citation UI.
- Loading/empty/error/stale/offline states.

## Stop Gate

Do not move to beta delivery polish until mobile completes topic -> source binding -> feed -> cited summary.

## Next Transition

Move to `05-realtime-delivery` when the mobile core loop is usable without developer help.
